Privacy & Data

DropSift @VERSION@ (@BUILD@) · Friends beta · 18 October 2026

Local processing

DropSift runs transcription, text extraction, and AI generation on your Mac. The app does not send your questions, documents, or recordings to a cloud inference service. AI can still make mistakes.

iCloud is a network service

When the library is in iCloud Drive, Apple can sync your recordings, transcripts, notes, summaries, extracted information, and conversations to other devices signed into your account. This is separate from local inference. iCloud protection and sharing depend on your Apple account and system settings.

Settings shows your library path. Anyone who can access that folder, its shared copies, or your account may be able to read its contents. DropSift does not add its own password or encryption layer.

Model downloads

Models download separately from the app. Downloads can contact Hugging Face, model publishers, and QVAC model registries. QVAC may use peer-assisted delivery of public model files. Those services or peers can see network metadata such as your IP address. Model downloads do not require sending your private library or prompts.

Downloaded model files stay in the local model cache, rather than your iCloud library. Each model has its own license. A model's license is separate from the app's source-code licenses.

Permissions and recording

Microphone access captures your voice. System-audio access captures audio played by the Mac, which can include other apps and notifications. Meeting detection watches local audio-use signals and asks before starting a recording. It does not guarantee that a meeting has started or ended.

Storage and deletion

The library uses ordinary files. Removing DropSift from Applications does not remove the library or model cache. Use the app's deletion controls carefully. Deletions in an iCloud library can sync to other devices. Keep a separate backup of important material.

Diagnostics and feedback

DropSift does not provide an automatic analytics or crash-upload service. macOS may collect system diagnostics according to your system settings. Processing logs can contain file names and error details. If you choose to share logs or examples, review them first and send them privately.

Private testing

This build is intended for a small group of testers. It has not received a full security, privacy, or cross-device release audit. Do not rely on generated text as the sole record of an important conversation.
